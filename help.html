How LearnHub works

Enrolling in a course

Create a free account, browse Courses, add a course to your cart and check out. You are enrolled immediately and the course appears under My Courses. The first lesson of every course is a free preview — you can watch and read it before enrolling.

Learning a lesson

Each lesson has an explanation, a flowchart, a free tutorial video, a quiz and practice tasks. Mark a lesson complete when you finish it — you earn +10 XP per lesson, +5 XP per practice task, and a +10 XP bonus when your best quiz score on a lesson is 80% or more. Use the bookmark button (🔖) to save a lesson, the notes panel to keep your own notes, and the discussion section to ask a question — instructors and other learners can reply.

Learning paths

If you are not sure where to start, open Learning Paths and pick a goal — Python Developer, Web Developer, AI & ML Engineer or C & DSA Foundations. Each path lists the courses in the order you should take them.

Certificates

Finish 100% of a course and you earn a certificate with a unique code (for example LH-XXXXXXXX). The certificate is permanent, even if you later unenrol. Anyone can verify a code on the certificate verification page — share it on your resume or LinkedIn.

The tutor

Every lesson page has a tutor that answers questions using only the actual course content — it explains topics, walks through flowcharts, quizzes you in chat and gives hints on tasks. It never invents content beyond the course.

XP, streaks and badges

Your Dashboard shows your XP and level (one level per 200 XP), your daily streak (learn on consecutive days to keep it going), and badges for milestones such as your first lesson, 5 and 10 lessons, quiz mastery, 5 tasks, finishing a course, and 3- and 7-day streaks. The leaderboard ranks learners by XP — only your first name and last initial are shown.

Your account

Update your details on your Profile page. Your wishlist saves courses for later. If you forget your password, use the contact form below and an admin will help you reset it — passwords are stored as salted hashes and are never visible to anyone, including admins.

Frequently asked questions

See the FAQ page for answers about payments, refunds, certificates and more.

Still stuck?

Send us a message through the contact page and we will get back to you. For announcements about new courses and features, see the Announcements page.
