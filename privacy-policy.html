Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. What we collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address and a password (stored only as a salted hash — nobody, including admins, can see your password). When you learn we store your enrolments, lesson progress, quiz attempts, task completions, notes, bookmarks, questions, reviews and certificates, so your dashboard, progress and certificates work.

2. How we use it

Your data is used only to run LearnHub: signing you in, tracking your learning, issuing and verifying certificates, showing your rank on the leaderboard, and answering support messages. The public leaderboard shows only your first name and last initial — never your email.

3. What we do not do

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising. We do not collect payment card details on this site. Lesson videos are embedded from YouTube; watching them is subject to YouTube's own privacy policy.

4. Cookies and local storage

LearnHub keeps your sign-in token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. No tracking cookies are used for advertising.

5. Your choices

You can unenrol from a course at any time from My Courses (your certificates are kept). To delete your account and learning data, contact us through the contact page and we will remove it. Questions about this policy? See the Help page or contact us.
